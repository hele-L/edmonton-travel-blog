Edmonton at a Glance

Edmonton is a vibrant city that combines natural scenery with urban attractions, making it a great destination for short trips or weekend travel. The river valley offers extensive trails and viewpoints that feel surprisingly close to downtown. The city also has lively neighbourhoods with local restaurants, cafés, and shops that are easy to explore on foot.

What Visitors Enjoy

  • Going for a walk along the River Valley
  • Taking a 90-minute boat tour on the Riverboat with buffet for brunch or dinner
  • Visiting the Muttart Conservatory
  • Exploring the Fort Edmonton Park historic sites

Best Time to Visit

Edmonton is the most beautiful in the summer and fall. The weather is sunny and warm, making it perfect for outdoor activities. The fall season brings vibrant foliage, while the summer offers numerous festivals and events. However, winter can be quite cold, so visitors should dress warmly if they choose to visit during that time.

One-Day Itinerary

  1. Start your day with a boat ride with brunch buffet on River Valley boat.
  2. Take a walk along the River Valley trail to enjoy the scenic views.
  3. Take the LRT to visit Fort Edmonton Park. Enjoy a horse ride around the town
  4. Visit the Muttart Conservatory for a dose of greenery and beauty.
